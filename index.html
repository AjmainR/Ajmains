05 — Knowledge

Cyber Security Basics

Simple explanations of important concepts I’m learning.

CIA Triad

Confidentiality — keep data private.
Integrity — keep data accurate.
Availability — keep systems working when needed.

Ethical Hacking

Testing systems with permission to find weaknesses so they can be fixed before real attackers find them. Always legal and responsible.

Common Threats

Malware, phishing, ransomware, social engineering, and weak passwords are among the most common ways systems get compromised.

Defense Basics

Strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, regular updates, firewalls, and security awareness training form the first line of defense.

Network Security

Protecting data as it travels across networks. Understanding routers, switches, firewalls, and how traffic can be monitored and filtered.

AI in Security

AI helps detect unusual behavior faster, analyze large amounts of logs, and support security analysts — when used correctly and ethically.
